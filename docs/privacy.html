Privacy Policy

My Budget Spending Streak · last updated 20 August 2026

In short: your spending data never leaves your phone. The app has no account and no server. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the app free.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — amounts, optional notes, your budgets and currency — is saved only in local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us, because there is no "us" to transmit it to: the app has no backend server, no account system and no analytics.

Deleting the app removes this data permanently. You can also erase it at any time with Settings → Delete all data, or take a copy with Settings → Export my data.

Advertising

The app displays a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and basic device information. Google's handling of that data is described in their Advertising policy and Privacy Policy.

Your spending entries are never shared with advertisers. The advertising SDK has no access to what you log in the app.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time via Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • On iOS you will be asked whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining is fine — the app works identically and you will still see ads, just less targeted ones.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings under Privacy → Ads.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Permissions

The app requests internet access. That is used only to load advertisements — never to send your spending data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
